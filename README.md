# Music Store Sales Analysis Dashboard

## Overview
A data analytics project exploring music store sales, customer behavior, geographic performance, and music trends through interactive dashboard visualizations.

## Dashboards

### 1. Executive Overview
![Executive Overview](dashboards/executive-overview.png)

- Revenue, invoices, customers, tracks, and countries served.
- Revenue by country and customer city.
- Genre-wise revenue distribution and key business insights.

### 2. Customer & Geographic Analysis
![Customer and Geographic Analysis](dashboards/customer-geographic-analysis.png)

- Customer distribution by country and age group.
- City-wise customer analysis and genre preferences.
- Top customers by spending.

### 3. Music & Artist Analysis
![Music and Artist Analysis](dashboards/music-artist-analysis.png)

- Genre-wise revenue and track distribution.
- Top artists by revenue and number of tracks.
- Most-played tracks and listener demographics.
